How to play

☝️

Fingers down near the edge. Phone flat in the middle, one finger each on your side of the screen (the dotted spots show where). The big circle is the reel, not a button. Hold still and the game begins.

🟢

Only lift on green. Lift while it's rolling, or a split second after it turns green, and you're caught.

🐦

It flies? Lift before time's up, or you're caught. If everyone lifts, the slowest finger takes a strike. Two strikes and you're caught.

📸

Photo finish. If the slowest fingers are within 0.01 seconds of each other, they all take a strike. If everyone's that close, it's a dead heat and nobody does.

🐘

It doesn't fly? Freeze. Lift and you're caught, along with anyone who lifts in the same instant.

🐧

Watch for gotchas. Penguins, chicks, caterpillars, eggs: they look like they should fly, and they don't. More of them turn up as the levels climb.

🔄

UDO Reverse (switch it on in the intro). Now and then a round with a pink ring flips the rule: lift for what doesn't fly, freeze for what does.

😈

Caught? Do the dare. Don't like it? Roll another, or let the others give you one. Tap Dare done, then Continue.

🏆

Knockout (on unless you pick Endless). Everyone has 3 lives (2 with 6 or more players), shown as the pieces of your ring. Each catch costs one. Out of lives? You can come back once by doing a second dare the table picks. Last finger standing wins. If a round would knock out everyone left, it's sudden death and nobody goes out.

🎯

Spotlight. When the ring turns one player's colour, only they play. Everyone else freezes, whatever lands. Lift and you're caught.

⚡

Rapid fire. Three reels back to back with almost no pause. Keep your finger down.

🥚

Three easter eggs are hidden in here. All bird-brained. None of them help you win.

🚀

A new level every 3 rounds. The reel spins faster, the slow crawl gets shorter and you get less time to lift.

🥇

End game any time for awards you can share with the group.
