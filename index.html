How to play

☝️

Fingers down. One each, outside the circle, phone flat in the middle. Hold still and the game begins.

🟢

Only lift on green. Lift while it's rolling, or a split second after it turns green, and you're caught.

🐦

It flies? Lift before time's up, or you're caught. If everyone lifts, the slowest finger takes a strike. Two strikes and you're caught.

📸

Photo finish. If the slowest fingers are within 0.05 seconds of each other, they all take a strike. If everyone's that close, nobody does.

🐘

It doesn't fly? Freeze. Lift and you're caught, along with anyone who lifts in the same instant.

🔄

UDO Reverse (switch it on in the intro). Now and then a round with a pink ring flips the rule: lift for what doesn't fly, freeze for what does.

😈

Caught? Do the dare. Don't like it? Roll another, or let the others give you one. Tap Dare done, then Continue.

🥚

Three easter eggs are hidden in here. All bird-brained. None of them help you win.

⚡

Every 5 rounds it speeds up.

🏆

End game for awards you can share with the group.
